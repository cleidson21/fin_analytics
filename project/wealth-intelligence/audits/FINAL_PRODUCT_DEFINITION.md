# Final Product Definition — FINANALYTICS Wealth Intelligence
**Status:** PROPOSED
**Date:** 2026-09-08

---

## What FINANALYTICS Becomes

FINANALYTICS transforms from a system that records and classifies financial history into a **personal financial intelligence platform** — one that helps the user understand where they are, where they are going, and what deserves their attention.

It is not a collection of calculators. It is not a chatbot. It is a coherent, trustworthy financial decision-support system that respects the user's intelligence and the integrity of their data.

---

## The User Experience, Module by Module

### When the user opens the application

The first screen is not a list of transactions. It is a **Wealth Intelligence Overview** — an executive dashboard that answers in seconds:

- **R$ [X]** — your total wealth today
- **+[Y]%** — how your investments performed this month vs IBOV and CDI
- **R$ [Z]/month** — your passive income trajectory
- **[Goal name]: [progress]%** — your most important goal at a glance
- **[X] years until financial independence** — based on your current trajectory
- **"2 things deserve your attention today"** — intelligent alerts

The user understands their financial situation before reading a single transaction.

---

### When they open Financial Health

They see their **spending intelligence** — not just a pie chart of categories.

They see:
- Their savings rate this month vs their historical average
- Which expense categories are above their 6-month average and by how much
- A rolling trend that shows whether their spending pattern is improving
- "Your transportation spending is 22% above your average" — calculated, not invented
- Whether their current spending leaves room for investment

They leave knowing whether they are on track or drifting — and what specifically changed.

---

### When they open Goals

They see a **goal engine** — a structured view of their financial objectives.

Each goal shows:
- The target and the current progress, clearly
- How much they need to invest monthly to reach it by the target date
- Whether they are on track, at risk, or behind
- A projected completion date based on current contribution
- A simple scenario: "If you add R$200/month, you reach this goal 14 months earlier"

They can create a goal in 3 clicks. The system does the math.

---

### When they open Retirement / FI

They see a **clear, honest projection** of their path to financial independence.

The system shows:
- At the current trajectory, they reach FI at age [X]
- The portfolio they need to maintain their current lifestyle
- The gap between current wealth and that target
- Three scenarios: conservative, base, optimistic
- A sensitivity table: "What if my return is 1% lower? What if I retire 5 years earlier?"

The projections are deterministic — not AI guesses. The AI explains them in plain language.

The user understands, for the first time, exactly where the math puts them — and what they can actually change.

---

### When they open Portfolio Intelligence

They see **why their portfolio changed** — not just that it did.

- "This month: +R$3,400 total. Contributions: +R$2,000. Price appreciation: +R$1,600. Dividends: +R$280. FX impact: -R$480."
- Which assets contributed most and which dragged performance
- Whether their portfolio is drifting from their target allocation
- Sector and geographic exposure
- How they compare to IBOV and CDI since inception

They understand their portfolio — not just its current value.

---

### When they open Market Radar

They see **market signals relevant to their holdings** — not the entire market.

- Which of their assets moved significantly today
- Technical signals (RSI, moving averages) with clear labeling: "SIGNAL — not advice"
- Relevant news for assets they own
- SELIC and inflation context
- Currency impact on their BDR and global ETF positions

Every signal shows its source, its timestamp, and distinguishes fact from interpretation.

---

### When they open Watchlist

They see assets they are monitoring. They set alerts. They write their thesis. The system notifies them when a price target is reached or a news item mentions the asset.

---

### When the AI Briefing appears

The Wealth AI briefing is **not a chatbot response**. It is a structured, expert-quality financial briefing — daily, weekly, or monthly — generated from verified data.

It looks like this:

---
**Daily Briefing — September 8, 2026**
*Generated by AI · Based on data as of 7:00 AM · Powered by [model]*

**Portfolio:** Your portfolio gained R$ 1,240 (+0.4%) since yesterday, driven primarily by BBAS3 (+2.1%) and KNCR11 (+0.3%). VALE3 was the main detractor (-1.8%), consistent with iron ore price pressure reported in the news.

**Goals:** Your Emergency Reserve goal is 92% funded. At your current rate, you will reach 100% in approximately 6 weeks.

**Attention:** Your discretionary spending this month is tracking 18% above your August average. If this continues, your estimated September savings rate will fall below your 20% target.

**Market:** IBOV fell 0.6% yesterday. SELIC remains at X%. USD/BRL is at Y.

*All financial figures are calculated from verified data. Market interpretations are AI-generated and should not be considered investment advice.*

---

The user reads this in 90 seconds and knows what needs their attention. They feel informed, not overwhelmed.

---

## What FINANALYTICS Is NOT

- It is not an autonomous financial decision-maker
- It is not a trading system
- It is not a broker or financial advisor
- It is not a data fabricator — if data is unavailable, it says so
- It is not a chatbot that answers arbitrary questions
- It is not a system that overwrites user data

---

## The Final Standard

The completed product should feel like having a **senior personal financial analyst** who:
- Knows your complete financial history
- Monitors your portfolio daily
- Tracks your goals
- Reads relevant market news
- Prepares a clear, honest briefing every morning
- Answers "how am I doing?" with evidence, not opinions

And who never pretends to know what they don't.

That is FINANALYTICS Wealth Intelligence.
